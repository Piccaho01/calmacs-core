# Sprite Prompts — remaining 9 + boss

Owned by 🔵 **Nightcrawler** (Art lead). Ready-to-paste prompts for the proven
pipeline, so the sheets stay consistent with the 7 already in the vault.

## How to generate (from any device)
1. Open **ChatGPT** (image generation).
2. Upload the **style reference**: `public/sprites/characters/Helper.png`
   — or, if that's on the home PC, use any finished sheet from this repo's
   `sprites/` (e.g. `sprites/batman.webp`) as the style anchor.
3. Paste the **shared preamble** + one character block below.
4. Save the PNG → extract with
   `python3 scripts/extract-boss-sprite.py your-character.png Name-1`.
5. Drop the result into `sprites/` and add a card to `sprites.html`.

> 🔵 **Nightcrawler:** *Bamf!* Same style bible every time — 4 poses, one row,
> white background, no text. If a pose drifts, it goes back to the canvas.

## Shared preamble (paste before every character block)

> Pixel-art character sprite sheet in the **exact same style, proportions, outline
> weight, shading and palette approach as the reference image**. Cute-but-modern
> chibi proportions. **One horizontal row of 4 full-body poses**, in this order:
> **Front-Left · Rear-Right · Front-Right · Rear-Left**. Pure **white background**,
> evenly spaced, consistent size and ground line, **no text, no labels, no borders**.

## 1. 🕷️ Spider-Man — Scout / procurement
Classic red-and-blue Spider-Man suit with black web lines and the large white eye
lenses. Lithe, agile build, light on his feet, mid-energy stance. Youthful hero.

## 2. 🎃 Green Goblin — Chaos breaker
Green-skinned goblin in a purple hood and tunic with jagged edges, menacing grin,
pointed ears. Chaotic, hunched-forward energy. Villain of the Break team.

## 3. 🐍 Loki — Devil's advocate
Asgardian trickster in green and gold with black accents, long dark hair, the horned
golden helmet. Sly, theatrical posture, a knowing smirk. Regal but mischievous.

## 4. 🖤 Venom — Fixer / hardener
Massive black symbiote creature, glossy black body with white spider emblem, huge
white jagged eyes, long tongue. Hulking, protective, menacing stance. Break-team fixer.

## 5. 🎩 Alfred — Quartermaster / organizer
Elderly dignified butler in a crisp black tailcoat, white gloves, neat grey hair and
a thin moustache. Upright, composed posture, perhaps holding a silver tray. Calm, refined.

## 6. 🔵 Nightcrawler — Art lead
Blue-skinned mutant with a pointed demon-like tail, yellow eyes, dark hair, in a dark
red-and-black acrobat's suit. Agile, theatrical, mid-flourish pose. Swashbuckler energy.

## 7. 💚 Hal Jordan (Green Lantern) — World lead
Grounded test-pilot build in the green-and-black Lantern suit with the white gloves
and the glowing green ring/emblem on the chest. Confident, steady, practical stance.

## 8. 👁️ Uatu the Watcher — Lore lead
Tall, pale, bald cosmic being with an oversized head and solemn eyes, draped in a
heavy flowing toga/robe. Detached, still, omniscient presence. Grave and imposing.

## 9. 🦝 Rocket Raccoon — Systems lead
Small bipedal raccoon in an orange-and-blue flight/jumpsuit, cocky stance, maybe a
gadget or tool in hand. Fast, scrappy, clever-engineer energy. Big expressive eyes.

## 10. 🫅 Boss — Calmac (you)
Young man, late teens / early twenties, with short tousled wavy dirty-blond hair, a
light faint moustache, light stubble, and small stud earrings. Calm, confident,
slightly cocky expression — this is the boss the heroes look up to. **Fit (streetwear
"baggy" style):** an oversized plain grey hoodie over a white tee, baggy light-grey
sweatpants, and clean sneakers; optionally a grey patterned durag or beanie. Give him
one subtle "sky-god / commander" marker so he reads as the boss — a faint gold glow
or a small floating gold crown above the head — but keep the outfit the real hero.
Same chibi pixel style as the rest of the crew.
